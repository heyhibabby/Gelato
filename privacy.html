Privacy Policy

Gelato & Ice Cream Balancer (G-Balancer)
Last updated: 01/09/26
App Store search name: Gelato & Ice Cream Balancer · Home-screen name: G-Balancer · Subtitle: Precise Visual Recipe Balancer

1. Summary (plain English)

  • We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information.
  • The App has no accounts, no login, no cloud sync, no servers of ours.
  • Everything you create — recipes, custom ingredients, settings, language, theme — is stored only on your own device.
  • There is no advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no cookies used for tracking.
  • Files you export (PDF, Excel/CSV, JSON) are written to your device and go only where you send them.

2. Who We Are

The App is developed and operated by Nitin Shah ("we," "us," "our"), 15, Gurukul Society, Nr. Circuit House, Shahibaug, Ahmedabad – 380004, Gujarat, India. Contact: G-Balancer@outlook.com. For the purposes of the EU/UK GDPR we act as the data controller only in respect of information you voluntarily email us.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect or receive: your name, email address, phone number, postal address, payment details, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, advertising IDs, IP-based profiles, usage analytics, crash telemetry, or your recipes and ingredient data. The App contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no social-media trackers.

4. Information Stored on Your Device

To function, the App saves data in your device's local storage (browser localStorage or equivalent app storage). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us:

Stored locally Purpose
Recipes, batches, ingredient selections and notes So your work is still there when you reopen the App
Custom / edited ingredient database entries To keep your own ingredient data
Machine, storage cabinet, overrun, process and serving settings To remember your production setup
Interface preferences (language, light/system/dark theme, units °C/°F) To restore your chosen settings
Recently used values and UI state Convenience

You control this data. You can delete all of it at any time by using the App's reset/clear functions, or by clearing site/app data for the App in your browser or device settings. Deletion is permanent and irreversible — we hold no copy and cannot restore it.

5. Exports and Sharing

The App can generate PDF, Excel/CSV and JSON exports of your recipes, labels, costings and ingredient database. These files are produced entirely on your device and saved to your device's storage. If you then email, print, upload or share them, that transfer is under your control and governed by the policies of whichever service you use.

6. Third-Party Resources

The App is designed to work offline. When an internet connection is available, it may load a small number of static third-party resources:

  • Web fonts (e.g. Google Fonts) for typography;
  • Document-generation libraries (e.g. jsPDF, SheetJS) from a public content delivery network (e.g. cdnjs/Cloudflare), used to build your PDF and spreadsheet exports.

When such a resource is requested, the provider's servers may automatically log technical data (such as IP address, browser type and time of request) as part of ordinary web-server operation. We receive none of this data and use it for nothing. These providers are not given any of your recipe or personal data. If you use the App with no network connection, or after these assets are cached, no such requests are made. The App makes no other network calls: no telemetry, no update pings, no user data uploads.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The App does not use cookies for advertising, profiling, or cross-site tracking. It uses only local storage strictly necessary for the App to remember your data and preferences, as described in Section 4.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your Rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is generally nothing for us to access, correct, export, or erase. You exercise practical control directly: your data lives on your device and is deleted when you clear it.

If you have emailed us (for support or a complaint), you may ask us to access, correct, or delete that correspondence at G-Balancer@outlook.com. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under the GDPR/UK GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, and to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority), the California CCPA/CPRA (we do not sell or share personal information and have not done so in the preceding 12 months), or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

10. Data Security and Retention

Because no data is transmitted to us, there is no server-side database to breach. Data stored locally is protected by your device's own security (screen lock, disk encryption, browser sandboxing). We recommend you keep your device secured and take your own exports as backups. We retain no data and therefore apply no retention period; any support email you send is kept only as long as needed to answer it.

11. International Transfers

No personal data is transferred by us to any country, because none is collected. Requests to third-party font/library CDNs (Section 6) may be served from data centres worldwide.

12. App Store Privacy Labels

Consistent with this policy, the App's store privacy disclosure is: Data Not Collected — no data is collected or linked to you, and no data is used for tracking.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date and, where appropriate, notified in-app. Continued use of the App after the update constitutes acceptance.
